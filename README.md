# Geometry Dash

## Why Should You Get It? (3 Reasons)

### The Game has a large variety of fun things to do
1. For Casual Players, playing the easier levels of the game and appreciating the art is very fun and inspiring
2. For Competitive Players, there are **thousands** of very difficult levels to test your skill
3. For Creative Players, there is the editor, that has a wide range of possibilities, allowing you to create whatever you want
