# Geometry Dash

## Why Should You Get It? (3 Reasons)

### The Game has a large variety of fun things to do
1. For Casual Players, playing the easier levels of the game and appreciating the art is very fun and inspiring

2. For Competitive Players, there are **thousands** of very difficult levels to test your skill
    - Insane Demons are a good start of competitive play
    - Low tier Extreme Demons are also a very strong place to start
    - List Demons you have to build up to by putting in a lot of time and effort
  
3. For Creative Players, there is the editor, that has a wide range of possibilities, allowing you to create whatever you want!

### Contests

Contests are occasionally hosted each year for the creator side of the community to compete for a position in a gauntlet

The most recent one of these contests was the *Duality Contest*  
These were the rules:
- Level had to between Harder Difficulty to Easy Demon difficulty
- The level had to more than a minute long
- The level had to have some correlation with the idea of Duality
